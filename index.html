How I work

A simple support workflow designed to keep customer communication clear and issues moving toward resolution.

1

Triage

Understand the request, urgency, and information already available.

2

Investigate

Review the issue, reproduce where possible, and check available documentation.

3

Resolve

Give the customer a clear, practical answer or the next required steps.

4

Escalate

When needed, provide the relevant context and technical details to the appropriate team.

5

Follow up

Keep the conversation organized and make sure the customer knows what happens next.
